About us

A family business since 1990

Shades Auto Repairs CC is a family-orientated business in the Durban North / Briardene region. Established by Mr. Haniff Shaik and later joined by his sons, the business grew from a small workshop into a market leader in the panel beating and spray painting industry.

Our highly competent and qualified team is committed to excellent service and workmanship, and we run an in-house training facility so our staff keep abreast of the latest technical advancements.
